## 📖 What Is ai-film-skills?

ai-film-skills is a complete toolkit that gives you **19 ready-to-use AI skills** for every stage of movie production. Whether you’re a YouTuber, short-film maker, or content creator, these skills help you plan, direct, and produce videos faster by using AI assistants like Claude, CodeBuddy, Codex, Trae, and WorkBuddy.

Think of it like a *Swiss Army knife* for filmmaking—each skill serves a specific job, from writing a director’s script to generating color palettes and storyboard layouts.

## 🧰 What’s Inside?

The 19 skills are grouped into five main categories:

### 🎥 Directing Skills
- **Scene Direction Generator** – Breaks down any script scene into camera angles, actor movements, and pacing.
- **Shot List Creator** – Turns your script into a production-ready shot-by-shot list.
- **Dialogue Pacing Advisor** – Suggests emotional beats and pauses for natural conversation.

### 📝 Storyboard Skills
- **Storyboard Layout Generator** – Creates 6-panel or 9-panel storyboard templates.
- **Continuity Checker** – Flags inconsistencies in props, lighting, or wardrobe across scenes.
- **Visual Flow Mapper** – Draws camera movement paths and transitions.

### 🎨 Asset Creation Skills
- **Prop & Set Dresser** – Lists period-accurate props and locations from your script.
- **Costume & Wardrobe Planner** – Suggests outfit color schemes per character.
- **Audio Asset List** – Generates soundtrack cues, foley effects, and ambient sound needs.

### 🖌️ Visual Design Skills
- **Color Palette Matcher** – Pulls cinematic color palettes from your moodboard images.
- **Title Sequence Designer** – Gives typography, motion, and layout ideas.
- **Lighting Blueprint** – Recommends 3-point lighting setups per scene type.

### 🎞️ Production & Research Skills
- **Crew List Generator** – Estimates crew roles needed for your shoot day.
- **Location Sourcing Sheet** – Provides checklist questions for location scouting.
- **Budget Breakdown Helper** – Roughly estimates daily costs for cast, crew, and equipment.
- **Reference Lookbook Collector** – Gathers visual anchors for mood and tone.
- **Post-Production Checklist** – Tracks VFX, sound mix, color grade, and final cut tasks.
- **Genre Convention Researcher** – Pulls tropes and formulas from similar films.
- **Audience Targeting** – Synthesizes viewer expectations based on niche.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### “I don’t see my AI assistant in the list.”
The skills are *plain-text instructions*, so they work with any modern AI chat interface. Just copy the text inside a skill file and paste it into your AI’s conversation window.

### “The ZIP file won’t extract.”
Windows sometimes blocks downloaded ZIPs. Right-click the ZIP → **Properties** → check **Unblock** → apply, then extract again.

### “No skills appear after import.”
Make sure you copied the entire content of each `.md` skill file into the correct "Skills" or "Custom Instructions" area of your AI tool. Some tools require you to save each one separately.

### “AI doesn’t follow the skill instructions well.”
Try putting the skill text at the very beginning of your conversation. This acts like a “brief” that the AI reads before responding.
